# AURA presentation flow

Open https://aura-college-workspace.streamlit.app/ in six tabs. Choose Student, Parent, Faculty, Department head, Governance and Learning workspace. Use the private login card beside the source delivery. Student021 and its linked Parent/mentor form the prepared presentation case. Student and Learning use the same account. The case is pending; approval is a human step.

1. **Student, Source records:** show file/sheet/cell provenance and untouched original values. Explain that original periods and source meanings remain unknown. The student sees one identity's supplied records.
2. **Learning workspace:** open a lesson, submit a short answer or attachment and inspect published feedback. Explain that saved learning activity becomes evidence; the raw answer is not sent to the cloud model.
3. **Faculty, Support reviews:** show the approved project policy. Create a review request, select Student021, Academic focus, the approved policy and Configured model review. Missing scope/domain/policy details produce questions. Saving refreshes the screen and selects the new plan.
4. **Lock review plan, Run review:** explain the four-source collectors, freshness/scope validation, policy computation, tagged lexical retrieval, anonymous Gemini action selection and evidence validation. Locking freezes scope and policy. Repeating execution does not duplicate completed jobs.
5. **Prepare evidence export:** download checkpoints and inspect actual mode, model, citations, validation/repairs and lineage. A quota failure records deterministic fallback; never present it as successful model inference.
6. **Governance:** show plan/job activity and recorded-run verification. A matching verification checks stored evidence and output hashes. It does not rerun the model or promise identical future generation.
7. **Faculty draft review:** inspect the support packet, citations, artifact, hash and revision. Edit a due date with a reason and inspect the refreshed version. The mentor personally decides whether to approve the exact displayed artifact. Do not claim an automated test is a real mentor sign-off.
8. **Student and Parent:** after the mentor's approval, show the published plan in both workspaces. Parent visibility follows the relationship and field grants. Withdraw/reinstate through the mentor's outcome controls if appropriate; history remains.
9. **Department head:** show the 321-student department scope and course oversight. The department view does not confer mentor draft approval authority.
10. **Fees and receipts:** demonstrate a project payment record or decline and its receipt. No money is collected. Finish with the audit trail and the preserved unknowns.

Source data and operational data are separate. The college supplied the source exports; additional project operations, relationships and accounts were constructed. Institution-confirmed definitions, account ownership, parent/mentor relationships and official policies remain prerequisites for real college operations.
